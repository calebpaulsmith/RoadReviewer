How the colors are decided
  1. Every road within the search radius is found, and the true distance from your point to each one is measured.
  2. The closest road decides red vs green — it's the road your point is on. Red if its class is federal-aid (Urban Minor Collector or greater), green otherwise. Urban vs rural comes from the 2020 Adjusted Census Urban Boundaries, never from the road data.
  3. Yellow only downgrades green — a green result turns yellow when something nearby could flip it to federal-aid: a federal-aid road within 30 ft of the closest road (GPS can't tell which one the point is on), any other federal-aid road inside the radius, a Minor Collector sitting right on an urban-boundary edge, or no road found at all. Red never downgrades — a federal-aid closest road stays red.
